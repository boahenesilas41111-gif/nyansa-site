Image generation

This one's live — it's calling Pollinations.ai, a free image API that doesn't need a secret key, so it works straight from the browser.

Heads up: free, keyless use is rate-limited (roughly one image per hour per visitor's IP address) — fine for a demo, not for real traffic. For anything client-facing, grab a free key at enter.pollinations.ai and add it to the request; it removes the watermark and raises the limit.

Video generation

Worth setting expectations here: this is the hardest of the three to offer for real.

Why it's different: providers like Runway, Luma, or Google's Veo charge per second of footage, jobs take minutes not seconds, and access is often gated or waitlisted. For a first version, it's usually smarter to launch with chat + images working, and add a "video — coming soon" state rather than promise something that isn't reliably live yet.
