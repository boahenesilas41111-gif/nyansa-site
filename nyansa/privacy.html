Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (handled securely through our authentication provider, Supabase). We also record how many images your account generates each day, and your subscription status.

What we don't collect

We do not store the images you generate, the text prompts you submit, or any payment card details — payments are processed entirely by Paystack, and we never see or store your card information.

How we use your information

  • To let you log in and use your account
  • To enforce the free daily image limit
  • To know whether your account has an active paid subscription
  • To contact you about your account if needed

Third parties involved

Nyansa uses Supabase for account authentication and data storage, Paystack for payment processing, and Pollinations.ai to generate the images themselves. Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy terms.

Data retention

Your account and usage data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email address associated with the Nyansa business account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address associated with the Nyansa business account.
