Frequently asked questions

How many images can I generate for free?

Every account gets 3 free image generations per day. The count resets daily.

How do I get unlimited images?

Once you've used your 3 free images for the day, an Upgrade button appears. It takes you to Paystack's secure checkout to start a monthly subscription. Your account updates automatically once payment goes through.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions can be managed and cancelled directly through Paystack — you'll get a receipt email from Paystack after subscribing with a link to manage your subscription.

Do I need an account to use Nyansa?

Yes — a free email sign-up is required so we can track your daily free images and your subscription status.

What happens to the images I generate?

Images are generated on request and shown to you in the browser. Nyansa does not store or share the images you generate.

I have feedback or a problem with my account — who do I contact?

Reach out at the email address associated with the Nyansa business account, and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
